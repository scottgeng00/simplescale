Problems from Everywhere: Challenging Problems Inspired by Everyday Reading

Every problem in this collection began with something the author happened to read: a news story, a forum post, a product page, a biography, a recipe. The seed passage is quoted first. The problem that follows is original and at olympiad / AIME difficulty: it takes an idea, object or situation from the passage in an unexpected mathematical direction, rather than asking about the passage's own numbers. Each problem has a precise, checkable answer. Solutions are at the end of the book.
